01 • THE COMPETITION

More Than An Examination.

It is a test of knowledge, confidence, awareness, reasoning and the ability to perform under pressure.

500+

students can experience a competitive learning environment built around knowledge and participation.

Shivkund Yuva Sah Seva Samiti organises its annual competitive examination and quiz competition to encourage students to learn, participate and challenge themselves.

Students from Classes 6 to 10 are invited to participate in the programme. The competition begins with a written examination and selected students progress to the oral competition.
