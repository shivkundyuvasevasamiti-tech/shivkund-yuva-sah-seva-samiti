ABOUT THE COMMITTEE

Service, Education & Youth Development

Shivkund Yuva Sah Seva Samiti works with the aim of encouraging students and young people through meaningful educational, cultural and community activities.

Our annual programme brings together academic excellence, sporting spirit, cultural expression and quiz-based learning in one shared celebration.

OUR ANNUAL PROGRAMME

Three Days of Learning, Competition & Celebration

Every August, Shivkund comes alive with a programme designed to develop students academically, physically and creatively.

13 AUGUST
01 / SPORTS EXCELLENCE 13 AUGUST

Sports Excellence

The annual programme begins with a sports competition for students of Middle School, Kurmi Tola.

Through sports, students learn the value of teamwork, discipline, perseverance and healthy competition.

SPORTS • TEAMWORK • DISCIPLINE Building confidence through participation.
02 / ACADEMIC EXCELLENCE 14 AUGUST

Preliminary Examination

Students from Classes 6 to 10 participate in the annual preliminary examination, creating an opportunity to demonstrate their knowledge and preparation.

The examination encourages students to study with purpose and develop a healthy spirit of academic competition.

CLASSES 6–10 • ₹20 REGISTRATION The top 15 students are recognised and rewarded.

03 / CULTURAL PROGRAM & QUIZ 15 AUGUST

Cultural Program, Creativity & Quiz

The celebration continues with our cultural programme and quiz, giving students a platform to express their creativity, confidence, knowledge and artistic talent.

Celebrating Culture, Creativity & Talent
We sincerely appreciate every artist and participant whose dedication, enthusiasm and performance make this celebration memorable. Their contribution reminds us that education is not only about examinations, but also about nurturing creativity, culture and character.
